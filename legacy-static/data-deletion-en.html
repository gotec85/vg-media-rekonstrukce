Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 4, 2026

VG MEDIA API NEW processes ad account data solely based on access granted by the client via Meta Business Manager. It does not collect end-consumer data directly.

How to stop data processing

The fastest and most effective way: the client (ad account owner) removes the app's or VG Media's system user access in Business Settings → Partners or System Users → assigned assets. This immediately revokes the app's access to all of that account's data via the Meta API.

Requesting deletion of stored data

If you would like us to delete any data we have stored locally about your account (e.g., reporting records), email info@vgmedia.cz with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your company name / ad account ID. We process such requests within 30 days.
