Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This application (VG MEDIA API NEW, App ID 1673360010603671) is an internal tool of the agency VG Media (Martin Gottvald), used to manage clients' advertising campaigns on the Meta platform (Facebook, Instagram) via the Meta Marketing API.

What data we process

Through the app we access data from the Meta Business/Marketing API that our clients have granted us access to for the purpose of managing their ad accounts. This includes, in particular:

  • ad account, campaign, ad set and ad data,
  • performance and reporting metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, conversions, etc.),
  • product catalog data (for dynamic/catalog campaigns),
  • identifiers of Pages and Instagram accounts linked to the ad accounts.

The app does not process personal data of clients' end customers beyond the aggregated advertising data provided by the Meta Marketing API.

Purpose of processing

We use this data solely to:

  • analyze and optimize the performance of clients' ad campaigns,
  • create and manage ad campaigns, subject to client approval,
  • report results to clients.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties beyond what is necessary to provide our agency services (the Meta platform itself). We do not sell data.

Security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored securely and are not publicly accessible. Only authorized VG Media staff have access to the data.

Data retention

We retain data for the duration of the client relationship and for as long as necessary to meet contractual and legal obligations.

Data subject rights

In accordance with GDPR, data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict the processing of their personal data. Contact us at the email below with any questions.
